PAGE 2: THE PROBLEM (THE $28B MONTH-END BLINDSPOT)
================================================================================
Corporate finance departments at mid-market companies ($10M–$250M ARR) face 
four crippling friction points in their reconciliation process:

1. THE 15-DAY REPORTING BLINDSPOT
   Controllers and CFOs operate blind for the first two weeks of every month. 
   Cash moves in real-time, but financial books lag 10 to 18 days behind. 
   Critical cash runway, treasury reallocation, and budget decisions are made on 
   stale numbers.

2. MULTI-CURRENCY TIMING VARIANCE & FX DRIFT
   Global operations across North America, Europe, the UK, and Asia create 
   constant reconciliation breaks. Fluctuations between transaction booking 
   dates and bank settlement dates create unexplained phantom variances that 
   junior accountants spend days manually untangling.

3. PAYMENT LEAKAGE & OVERPAYMENTS
   Duplicate vendor invoices, accidental duplicate wires, and unnoticed price 
   hikes slip through spreadsheet checks. Mid-market companies lose 0.5% to 
   2.0% of their operational expenditure annually to payment leakage.

4. EXPENSIVE CPA DISCREPANCY INVESTIGATION
   When bank statement balances differ from general ledger balances by $2,500, 
   senior CPAs waste 4 to 8 hours cross-referencing thousands of line items to 
   find a single unposted wire or bank fee.

PAGE 8: FINANCIAL GUARDRAILS & ZERO-HALLUCINATION POLICY
================================================================================
Corporate finance demands 100% precision. LedgerAI enforces five non-negotiable 
engineering guardrails:

1. STRICT ZERO FLOATING-POINT ARITHMETIC
   All monetary amounts use Python `decimal.Decimal` with 4 decimal places for 
   balances and 6 decimal places for currency rates. IEEE 754 floating-point 
   rounding errors are strictly prevented.

2. DETERMINISTIC FINANCIAL CALCULATIONS
   LLMs are NEVER permitted to calculate, invent, or adjust balances. All ledger 
   math, discrepancies, and reconciliation scores are computed via deterministic 
   algorithms. LLMs are restricted to descriptive natural-language summaries.

3. MANDATORY HUMAN-IN-THE-LOOP CPA GATE
   No autonomous algorithm is permitted to write or post unapproved closing entries 
   directly to a live general ledger. All prepared entries require certified CPA 
   review and sign-off.

4. CRYPTOGRAPHIC IDEMPOTENCY
   Every ingestion computes a SHA-256 fingerprint from the account ID, date, 
   amount, and reference. Duplicate feeds can never enter the system.

5. IMMUTABLE SOX-COMPLIANT AUDIT TRAIL
   Every categorization override, manual pairing, and approval action is stored 
   in an append-only audit log with timestamp, user ID, and source IP address.
