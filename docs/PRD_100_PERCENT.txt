1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & PRODUCT VISION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
LedgerAI transforms corporate financial close from an arduous, error-prone 
month-end sprint into a continuous, real-time autonomous pipeline.

Traditional corporate accounting teams spend 10 to 18 business days after every 
month-end manually matching bank statement lines against general ledger records, 
converting multi-currency rates, untangling phantom discrepancies, and manually 
drafting closing journal entries.

LedgerAI delivers a complete autonomous bookkeeping operations agent:
1. Ingests multi-currency bank feeds in real-time with SHA-256 deduplication.
2. Normalizes foreign exchange using high-precision Decimal arithmetic (ASC 830).
3. Automatically classifies expenses into standard GAAP charts of accounts with 
   confidence scoring and continuous human accountant feedback learning.
4. Executes a 3-tier reconciliation engine (exact, fuzzy, and partial matching).
5. Intercepts payment fraud and anomalies (72-hour duplicates, Z-score outliers, 
   and off-hour wire spikes).
6. Prepares balanced double-entry month-end draft journal entries with FX gain/loss 
   adjustments and strict CPA approval gates.
7. Provides a ground-truth conversational AI finance assistant answering complex 
   ledger queries without mathematical hallucination.
8. Delivers audit-ready close reports, expense breakdowns, and NetSuite/QuickBooks 
   export packages.


2. THE PROBLEM & BUSINESS OPPORTUNITY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Corporate reconciliation suffers from four core operational bottlenecks:

1. Reporting Latency (The Month-End Blindspot):
   Cash visibility is delayed by up to 3 weeks following calendar close. 
   Corporate controllers cannot make real-time treasury decisions because 
   financial books lag behind bank transactions.

2. Foreign Exchange Drift & Conversion Variances:
   Enterprises operating across multiple jurisdictions (US HQ, European 
   Subsidiary, UK Office, India Tech Center) suffer frequent breaks due to 
   exchange rate shifts between invoice booking dates and bank settlement dates.

3. Payment Leakage & Overpayment Risk:
   Duplicate vendor invoices, accidental double-wires, and unannounced software 
   subscription price increases slip past spreadsheet reviews, costing 
   companies 0.5% to 2.0% of annual operating expenditure.

4. Discrepancy Investigation Costs:
   Finding the root cause of an unexplained $2,500 variance across 10,000 line 
   items costs between 4 and 8 hours of senior CPA manual effort.

At a target SaaS subscription of $500–$1,500/month, LedgerAI reduces manual 
matching workload by over 80%, accelerates financial close from 15 business days 
to under 48 hours, and enforces double-entry mathematical integrity.


3. FULL 100% FUNCTIONAL SCOPE DELIVERED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The complete 100% production system delivers all 8 core functional modules:

3.1 MODULE 1: MULTI-CURRENCY BANK INGESTION & DEDUPLICATION
- Structured CSV file upload and simulated banking feeds.
- Cryptographic SHA-256 deduplication hashing:
    hash(bank_account_id + date + amount + reference)
- Zero duplicate transactions allowed into the database.
- Multi-bank account synchronization support across 4 banking entities.

3.2 MODULE 2: DETERMINISTIC MULTI-CURRENCY PRECISION ENGINE
- Fixed-point Decimal arithmetic: Decimal(18, 4) for amounts, Decimal(18, 6) for rates.
- Strict Zero Floating-Point Arithmetic policy prevents penny-rounding drift.
- ASC 830 / IAS 21 foreign currency conversion rules implemented.
- Preserves original amount, original currency, normalized USD value, and historical FX rate.

3.3 MODULE 3: AI-POWERED TRANSACTION CATEGORIZATION
- NLP classification into US GAAP charts of accounts:
    * Operating Expenses (SaaS, Cloud Infrastructure, Rent, Utilities, Travel)
    * Payroll & Compensation
    * Revenue & Inbound Customer Receipts
    * Intercompany & Treasury Transfers
- Confidence scoring for every classification:
    * High confidence (>= 85%): Auto-categorized.
    * Low confidence (< 85%): Routed to CPA human review queue.
- Continuous learning loop: Accountant manual reclassifications train merchant memory.

3.4 MODULE 4: 3-TIER RECONCILIATION MATCHING ENGINE
- Tier 1 (Exact Match): 1-to-1 match on normalized amount, date, and reference (Score 1.00).
- Tier 2 (Fuzzy Match): Variance <= $0.50, date window <= 4 days, description similarity >= 0.55.
- Tier 3 (Partial Match): Detects wire transfer fees and merchant FX deductions (variance <= 5%).
- Interactive manual pairing interface for unresolved records.
- Discrepancy Diagnostic Engine identifying statement vs. book balance variances with 
  root-cause explanations (e.g. diagnosing the $2,500 unbooked SVB wire).

3.5 MODULE 5: ANOMALY & FRAUD DETECTION GATEWAY
- 72-Hour Duplicate Payment Interception:
    * Flags identical or near-identical wires sent to the same vendor within 72 hours.
- Statistical Z-Score Outlier Detection:
    * Computes rolling mean and standard deviation per merchant category.
    * Flags transactions with Z > 3.0 as critical spending outliers.
- Off-Hour / Weekend Transfer Alerts:
    * Flags unusual high-value wires executed on weekends or holidays.
- Anomaly Investigation Hub:
    * Plain-English anomaly explanations.
    * Risk levels: Critical, High, Medium, Low.
    * Investigation workflow: Open, Investigating, Resolved, False Positive.

3.6 MODULE 6: BALANCED DOUBLE-ENTRY JOURNAL GENERATOR
- Autonomous Draft Closing Journal Creation:
    * Strict double-entry invariant: Sum(Debits) === Sum(Credits).
    * Balancing cash lines generated against Operating Cash (Account 1000).
    * Realized FX gain/loss adjustment lines generated upon foreign bill settlement.
- Mandatory Human-in-the-Loop CPA Sign-Off Gate:
    * Strict safety rule: Unapproved draft journals are NEVER posted to live books.
    * CPAs can review line items, modify debit/credits, approve, or reject.
- General Ledger Export Formats:
    * One-click export to NetSuite CSV and QuickBooks Online IIF/CSV formats.

3.7 MODULE 7: CONVERSATIONAL AI FINANCE ASSISTANT

    * Translates natural language questions into deterministic SQL queries against the ledger.
    * Answers: "Why is there a $2,500 difference in our SVB balance?",

3.8 MODULE 8: FINANCIAL REPORTS & MONTH-END CLOSE

5. FINANCIAL GUARDRAILS & DATA SAFETY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Zero Floating-Point Arithmetic:
   All operations use Python `decimal.Decimal`. Storing or calculating monetary balances 
   using IEEE 754 floating-point numbers is strictly prohibited.

2. Idempotent Ingestion Hash:
   Unique database index enforces SHA-256 fingerprinting so no transaction can be 
   double-counted.

3. Double-Entry Mathematical Invariant:
   Sum(Debits) - Sum(Credits) === 0.0000. Any entry failing this check is rejected.

4. Human-in-the-Loop Safeguard:
   No autonomous agent is permitted to post unapproved journal entries to live 
   general ledgers without CPA approval.

5. Immutable SOX-Compliant Audit Trail:
   Every manual override, category correction, anomaly resolution, and journal 
   approval is permanently recorded in the immutable `audit_logs` table.
