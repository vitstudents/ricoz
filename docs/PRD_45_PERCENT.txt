1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & PRODUCT VISION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
LedgerAI is an autonomous financial operations platform designed to eliminate 
manual month-end reconciliation and bookkeeping for corporate finance teams.

Traditional corporate accounting teams spend 10 to 18 business days after every 
month-end manually matching bank statement lines against general ledger records, 
converting multi-currency rates, and chasing down unexplained variances.

The Phase 1 Foundation Milestone (45% Scope) establishes the autonomous 
processing core:
1. Multi-currency bank feed ingestion with SHA-256 deduplication.
2. High-precision decimal currency normalization (USD, EUR, GBP, INR).
3. AI-powered transaction categorization with confidence scoring.
4. A deterministic 3-tier reconciliation engine (exact, fuzzy, and partial matching).
5. A streamlined 3-view web dashboard for accountants (Overview, Transactions, 
   and Reconciliation Center).


2. THE PROBLEM & BUSINESS OPPORTUNITY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Corporate reconciliation suffers from three core operational bottlenecks:

1. Manual Reconciliation Drag:
   Finance teams spend over 40 hours each month manually matching spreadsheet 
   rows from different bank accounts against general ledger entries.

2. Multi-Currency Timing Variance:
   Transactions booked in foreign currencies (EUR, GBP, INR) frequently drift 
   against bank settlements due to exchange rate movements, causing phantom 
   discrepancies.

3. Unexplained Discrepancy Delays:
   Tracing an unexplained variance between bank balances and internal books 
   typically consumes hours of senior CPA investigation time.

At a target SaaS subscription of $500–$1,500/month, LedgerAI reduces manual 
matching workload by over 75% in Phase 1, accelerating close speed while 
ensuring zero mathematical drift.

3.1 IN-SCOPE: Verified Features for 45% Milestone
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Multi-Feed Ingestion & Deduplication:
   - Ingestion of multi-currency bank feeds via structured CSV uploads and simulated 
     banking API feeds.
   - Idempotent SHA-256 deduplication hashing:
     hash(bank_account_id + date + amount + reference)
   - Zero duplicate transactions allowed into the database.

2. Deterministic Multi-Currency Precision Engine:
   - Fixed-point Decimal arithmetic (4 decimal places for amounts, 6 for rates).
   - Zero floating-point drift across USD, EUR, GBP, and INR.
   - Accurate preservation of original transaction amount, original currency, 
     and normalized USD base value.

3. AI Transaction Categorization & Review Queue:
   - Automated categorization into standard GAAP chart of accounts.
   - Confidence scoring for every classification:
     * High confidence (>= 85%): Auto-categorized.
     * Review queue (< 85%): Flagged for accountant verification.
   - Accountant feedback loop: manual reclassifications update merchant memory.

4. 3-Tier Reconciliation Engine:
   - Tier 1 (Exact Match): Matches on exact amount, reference, and date.
   - Tier 2 (Fuzzy Tolerance Match): Matches with variance <= $0.50, date <= 4 days.
   - Tier 3 (Partial Match): Identifies fee/FX deductions (variance <= 5%).
   - Manual pairing interface for unmatched items.

5. Discrepancy Diagnostic Analyzer:
   - Calculates mathematical variance between statement balance and book balance.
   - Explains discrepancy root causes (e.g. unbooked $2,500 outgoing wire).

6. Focused 3-View Dashboard:
   - Overview: Total cash balance, transaction volume, reconciliation completion %.
   - Transactions: Searchable table, category filters, currency tags, confidence scores.
   - Reconciliation Center: Matched vs. unmatched pairs, discrepancy drilldown.

5. FINANCIAL GUARDRAILS & DATA SAFETY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Zero Floating-Point Arithmetic:
   All calculations use Python `decimal.Decimal`. IEEE 754 floating-point 
   arithmetic is strictly prohibited.

2. Idempotent Storage:
   Unique hash index ensures identical files or transactions can never be 
   double-counted.

3. Immutable Audit Trail:
   Every transaction import, category correction, and manual match is logged 
   with timestamp and user ID.
