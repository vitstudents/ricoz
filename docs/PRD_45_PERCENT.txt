1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & PRODUCT VISION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
LedgerAI transforms corporate financial close from an arduous, error-prone 
month-end marathon into a continuous, real-time autonomous pipeline.

Traditional corporate accounting teams spend between 10 and 18 business days 
at the end of every calendar month manually matching bank statements against 
general ledger books, reconciling foreign currency variance, and investigating 
unexplained balance discrepancies.

LedgerAI continuously ingests transactions from multi-currency bank feeds, 
enforces strict idempotent deduplication, normalizes multi-currency rates with 
high-precision Decimal arithmetic, categorizes expenses according to US GAAP / 
IFRS standards, detects duplicate payments and statistical outliers, and prepares 
balanced double-entry draft journal entries ready for certified accountant (CPA) 
review and approval.


2. THE PROBLEM & BUSINESS OPPORTUNITY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The corporate reconciliation workflow has four major structural pain points:

1. Reporting Latency (The Month-End Blindspot):
   Cash visibility is delayed by up to 3 weeks following the close of a month. 
   Corporate controllers cannot make real-time treasury decisions because 
   financial books lag behind bank transactions.

2. Foreign Exchange Drift & Conversion Variances:
   Enterprises operating across multiple jurisdictions (e.g. US Headquarters, 
   European Subsidiary, UK Commercial Office, India Technology Center) suffer 
   frequent reconciliation breaks due to exchange rate shifts between invoice 
   booking dates and settlement dates.

3. Financial Leakage & Overpayment Risk:
   Duplicate vendor invoices, accidental double-wires, and unannounced software 
   subscription price increases regularly slip past manual spreadsheet reviews, 
   costing companies 0.5% to 2% of annual operating expenditure.

4. High Labor Cost of Discrepancy Investigation:
   Finding the root cause of an unexplained $2,500 variance across 10,000 line 
   items costs between 4 and 8 hours of senior CPA manual effort.

LedgerAI addresses this at an accessible $500–$1,500/month price point, offering 
a payback period under 30 days by cutting 80% of manual bookkeeping hours and 
preventing payment leakage.

3.1 IN-SCOPE: What is Delivered in the 45% Milestone
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Multi-Feed Ingestion & Deduplication:
   - Ingestion of multi-currency bank feeds via CSV upload and simulated banking APIs.
   - Idempotent SHA-256 deduplication hashing preventing duplicate records.

2. Deterministic Multi-Currency Precision Engine:
   - Fixed-point Decimal arithmetic (4 decimal places for amounts, 6 for rates).
   - Realized and unrealized foreign exchange gain/loss calculation under ASC 830.

3. Intelligent AI Categorization & Continuous Learning Loop:
   - Automated categorization into standard GAAP chart of accounts.
   - High confidence (>= 85%) auto-accepted; low confidence (< 85%) flagged for CPA.
   - Accountant feedback loop: manual reclassifications train merchant memory.

4. 3-Tier Reconciliation Matching Engine:
   - Tier 1 (Exact): 1-to-1 match on amount, reference, and date (Confidence: 1.0).
   - Tier 2 (Fuzzy): Matches with variance <= $0.50, date <= 4 days, text >= 0.55.
   - Tier 3 (Partial): Identifies fee deductions (wire fees, FX delta <= 5%).

5. Discrepancy Diagnostic Analyzer:
   - Calculates mathematical variance between statement balance and book balance.
   - Produces natural language explanation of root cause (e.g. unbooked $2,500 wire).

6. Anomaly & Fraud Interception Gate:
   - Flags duplicate payments sent to the same vendor within 72 hours.
   - Z-score outlier detection on debit amounts (Z > 3.0 flagged as critical).
   - Flags unusual weekend/off-hour high-value corporate transfers.

7. Balanced Double-Entry Month-End Journal Preparation:
   - Autonomous preparation of draft closing entries where Debits == Credits.
   - Balancing cash lines generated against Account 1000 (Operating Cash).
   - Mandatory CPA sign-off: Unapproved entries are strictly blocked from live ledgers.
   - General Ledger export to NetSuite and QuickBooks CSV formats.

8. Ground-Truth AI Financial Assistant:
   - Deterministic SQL query resolution answering discrepancy and expense inquiries.
   - Zero LLM arithmetic hallucination on financial balances.

9. Complete 8-Page Web Application:
   - Overview Dashboard, Transactions Table, Reconciliation Center, Anomaly 
     Detection Hub, Journal Entries Studio, AI Assistant, Reports, and Settings.

5. TECHNICAL SPECIFICATIONS & FINANCIAL GUARDRAILS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Zero Floating-Point Arithmetic Policy:
   All currency operations in Python use the `decimal.Decimal` module. Storing or 
   calculating monetary balances using IEEE 754 floating-point numbers is 
   strictly prohibited to prevent penny-rounding accumulation errors.

2. Idempotent Ingestion Hash:
   DedupHash = SHA256( BankAccountID + Date(YYYY-MM-DD) + Amount + Reference )
   A unique database index enforces that no transaction can be ingested twice.

3. Double-Entry Invariant:
   Sum(Debits) - Sum(Credits) === 0.0000
   Any journal entry failing this mathematical check is rejected by the system.

4. Human-in-the-Loop Safeguard:
   No autonomous agent is permitted to post unapproved journal entries to live 
   general ledgers. All AI-prepared journals enter the state machine as 'draft' 
   and require explicit CPA authentication to move to 'approved'.

5. Comprehensive Audit Trail:
   Every manual override, category reclassification, anomaly resolution, and journal 
   approval is permanently recorded in the immutable `audit_logs` table with 
   user ID, timestamp, and IP address.
