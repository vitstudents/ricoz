PART 1: WHAT IS DONE (PHASE 1 FOUNDATION - 45% SCOPE)
================================================================================

[DONE] 1. MULTI-CURRENCY BANK INGESTION & DEDUPLICATION
  * Support for multi-currency feeds across USD (Base), EUR, GBP, and INR.
  * Structured CSV file upload with automated column mapping.
  * SHA-256 idempotent transaction hashing:
      hash(bank_account_id + date + amount + reference)
  * 100% duplicate transaction prevention at the database constraint level.
  * Multi-bank account synchronization support (4 accounts active).

[DONE] 2. DETERMINISTIC FINANCIAL PRECISION ENGINE
  * Strict Zero Floating-Point Arithmetic policy:
      - All amounts stored as Decimal(18, 4).
      - All exchange rates stored as Decimal(18, 6).
  * ASC 830 / IAS 21 foreign currency conversion rules implemented.
  * Simultaneous preservation of:
      - Original transaction amount and original currency.
      - Normalized base functional currency value (USD).
      - Historical conversion rate applied at booking time.

[DONE] 3. AI-POWERED TRANSACTION CATEGORIZATION
  * Natural language categorization into standard US GAAP chart of accounts:
      - Operating Expenses (SaaS, Cloud Infrastructure, Rent, Utilities)
      - Payroll & Contractor Compensations
      - Revenue & Inbound Customer Receipts
      - Intercompany & Treasury Transfers
  * Confidence scoring assigned to every categorization:
      - High Confidence (>= 85%): Automatically categorized.
      - Low Confidence (< 85%): Routed to CPA human review queue.
  * Continuous accountant feedback loop:
      - Manual reclassifications train merchant categorization memory.

[DONE] 4. 3-TIER AUTOMATED BANK RECONCILIATION ENGINE
  * Tier 1 (Exact Match):
      - 1-to-1 match on exact normalized amount, date, and reference.
      - Assigns Confidence Score 1.00.
  * Tier 2 (Fuzzy Match):
      - Tolerance window of +/- $0.50 amount variance and +/- 4 calendar days.
      - Text similarity threshold >= 0.55 on merchant descriptions.
  * Tier 3 (Partial Match):
      - Intercepts and identifies wire transfer fees and FX fee deductions (<= 5%).
  * Manual Pairing Interface:
      - Allows CPAs to search, link, and reconcile unmatched entries manually.

[DONE] 5. DISCREPANCY DIAGNOSTIC ANALYZER
  * Real-time calculation of mathematical variance:
      Discrepancy = Statement Balance - Calculated General Ledger Balance
  * Autonomous root-cause explanation engine:
      - Example: Detects the $2,500 variance in Silicon Valley Bank account 
        caused by an unbooked outgoing vendor wire from October 28.

[DONE] 6. STREAMLINED 3-VIEW WEB DASHBOARD (REACT 19 + TAILWIND CSS)
  * View 1: Overview Dashboard
      - Real-time total cash balance across all active bank accounts.
      - Ingestion transaction volume counter (1,054 transactions).
      - Continuous reconciliation health gauge (91.2% reconciled).
      - Discrepancy alert cards with direct drill-down links.
      - Multi-currency bank account summary table.
  * View 2: Transactions Feed
      - Searchable, filterable transaction table.
      - Filters by category, currency (USD, EUR, GBP, INR), and date.
      - AI category pill with confidence tags and CPA review queue filters.
  * View 3: Reconciliation Center
      - Tabbed interface: Matched (821), Partial (16), Unmatched (217).
      - Side-by-side comparison of bank record vs internal ledger record.
      - 1-click manual reconciliation action.

[DONE] 7. PRODUCTION INFRASTRUCTURE & REPOSITORIES
  * Production Deployment on Vercel:
      - Active URL: https://work-hazel-one.vercel.app
      - Static assets served via Vercel Edge CDN with sub-second loading.
      - Serverless Python entrypoint (api/index.py) with SQLite WAL mode.
  * Source Code Pushed to GitHub:
      - Repository: https://github.com/vitstudents/ricoz (Branch: main).
  * Automated Verification:
      - 9 of 9 automated Pytest tests passing.
      - Evaluated on 1,054 transactions benchmark (195 tx/sec throughput).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PHASE 2: FINANCIAL INTELLIGENCE & MONTH-END CLOSE (NEXT 25% → 70% TARGET)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

[REMAINING] 1. ANOMALY & FRAUD DETECTION ENGINE
  * 72-Hour Duplicate Payment Interception:
      - Flags identical or near-identical wires sent to the same vendor within 72h.
  * Statistical Z-Score Outlier Detection:
      - Computes rolling mean and standard deviation per merchant category.
      - Automatically flags transactions exceeding Z > 3.0 as critical outliers.
  * Off-Hour / Weekend Transfer Alerts:
      - Intercepts large wires executed outside standard business hours.
  * Anomaly Investigation Hub:
      - Detailed risk scoring (Critical, High, Medium, Low).
      - Plain-English anomaly explanations.
      - Workflow state: Open, Investigating, Resolved, False Positive.

[REMAINING] 2. BALANCED DOUBLE-ENTRY JOURNAL GENERATOR
  * Autonomous Draft Journal Creation:
      - Generates debit and credit entry lines based on reconciled transactions.
      - Strict double-entry invariant: Sum(Debits) === Sum(Credits).
      - Automatically creates balancing cash entries against Account 1000.
  * Foreign Exchange Adjustments:
      - Generates realized FX gain/loss entries upon foreign bill settlement.
      - Generates period-end unrealized FX balance sheet adjustments.
  * Human-in-the-Loop CPA Sign-Off Gate:
      - Strict safety rule: Unapproved draft journals are NEVER posted to live books.
      - Review, edit line items, approve, or reject interface for accountants.
  * General Ledger Exports:
      - One-click export to NetSuite CSV and QuickBooks Online IIF/CSV formats.

[REMAINING] 3. CONVERSATIONAL AI FINANCE ASSISTANT
  * Natural language conversational interface embedded directly in the workspace.
  * Zero-Hallucination Ground-Truth Query Engine:
      - Resolves queries by compiling natural language into deterministic SQL.
      - Never allows LLM to invent or estimate monetary balances.
  * Sample Supported Queries:
      - "Why is there a $2,500 difference in our Silicon Valley Bank account?"
      - "Show all unmatched software subscription payments from October."
      - "How much did we spend on AWS and Google Cloud last month?"
      - "Identify unusual vendor payments this quarter."

[REMAINING] 4. MONTH-END CLOSE FINANCIAL REPORTING
  * Monthly Close Summary Package:
      - Total Inflow, Total Outflow, Net Burn, and Operating Cash Runway.
  * Expense Breakdown by Department and Chart of Account.
  * Foreign Currency Exposure & Net Realized Gain/Loss Summary.
  * Audit-ready reconciliation report package with 1-click PDF and CSV exports.
